- Sound effects across the app: correct and wrong answers in every game, a pop when you find a word, and sounds for finishing a game, lesson, story or flashcard deck.
- Streaks, badges and level-ups now celebrate with sound, and buttons, tabs and flashcard flips give a soft click.
