- New updates now pop up as soon as they're found, with what's changed and a one-tap download. Choose "Later" and a small reminder stays on Home until you update or close it. Required updates can't be skipped.
- If you have a test build that can't install the official update, the app now explains why and takes you to the download page.
- Streaks are fairer: when no review words are due, playing three games is enough to keep your streak, and finishing a game and a review at the same moment no longer celebrates twice.
- Your streak reminder now arrives at the time you chose in Settings, and turning it off stops it completely.
- The Word of the Day switch in Settings now sends a word every morning at 8:00 AM, opening on that word's page. Home and the Library always show the same word of the day.
- The notification inbox starts empty and shows only messages that were actually sent, with times like "5 min ago" and "Yesterday".
- The guided tour now pauses a little longer on each tab.
