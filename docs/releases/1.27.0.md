- Leaving a game no longer loses your round. Every game saves after each answer and picks up where you stopped when you open that level again. Levels with a game in progress are marked, and the quit message tells you your progress is kept.
- Every game's results screen has Start over, which plays the level again from the beginning with all your hints back.
- Word Wheel hints now give a clue: how long a hidden word is and what it means, instead of uncovering a letter. The clue stays until you find that word. "Show meanings" is gone, and the board no longer shrinks while you play.
- Word Match's answer panel no longer has a second speaker button. The word at the top still plays its recording.
- Home's greeting now shows in full beside your picture.
- "Report an issue" is red wherever it appears, so it is easy to find.
- Titles and labels no longer lose words: "Sentence Order" in the game list, "words practised" on your profile, and long related words on a word's page.
- In the dark theme, stars you have not earned no longer look lit on the results screen.
