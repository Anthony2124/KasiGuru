# KasiGuru single-badge prompt: six visual tiers

User direction: 2026-09-30. Reference:
`design/assets/badges/six-tier-cartoon-reference.jpg`.

This supersedes the five-metal-tier concept's visual direction. Use Beginner,
Learner, Achiever, Expert, Master and Legend. Generate one badge per request.
Numeric thresholds and XP rewards require a separate six-stage balance revision;
the artwork prompt does not finalize those values.

## How to use

Fill in FAMILY, MILESTONE, TIER and CENTRAL SYMBOL, then send the filled prompt
with the reference image. For an existing family, also attach its chosen base
badge and use the upgrade instruction below. A mascot reference is useful only
when actually attached; the mascot's name alone does not define its appearance.

First Word is the entry milestone of Word Explorer. Its speech-bubble-and-star
symbol stays the same at higher tiers; the increasingly advanced milestones are
supplied by the app's badge catalog. Do not create a separate family for every
word-count threshold.

## Reusable generation prompt

```text
Create exactly ONE standalone achievement badge for KasiGuru, a language-learning
app. This is one stage of an evolving achievement family.

FAMILY: [WORD EXPLORER]
MILESTONE: [FIRST WORD]
REQUESTED TIER: [1 — BEGINNER]
CENTRAL SYMBOL: [A rounded speech bubble containing one large four-point sparkle.
The speech bubble communicates vocabulary and speaking; the sparkle communicates
a first achievement. No words, letters or numbers inside it.]

REFERENCES
- The attached six-badge reference defines the rounded construction, simple
  cartoon shading and increasing tier prestige. Use it as visual guidance;
  generate one original customized badge, not the reference grid.
- If a chosen badge from this same family is attached, preserve its central
  symbol, medallion proportions, icon placement and illustration style exactly.
- If a KasiGuru mascot reference is attached, match its rounded forms, clean
  edges, playful proportions and simple highlights. Do not put the mascot or any
  other character into the badge.

CONSTRUCTION AND STYLE
Use a front-facing circular medallion with a thick outer rim, a secondary inner
ring and a softly scalloped inner seal. Center one large achievement emblem in
the seal. Use smooth layered shapes, clean cartoon contours, broad color areas
and restrained dimensional highlights. Light comes softly from the upper left.
The result should feel friendly, collectible and polished, with the simplified
illustration style of the supplied reference.

Use the same medal body scale, border proportions, central symbol silhouette
and icon placement across all stages of this family. Upgrade the frame, color
and tier accessories while preserving that identity. Do not add extra objects
to the central symbol at higher tiers. The family emblem must remain the main
subject at every tier.

SIX-STAGE VISUAL SYSTEM
Only apply the instructions for REQUESTED TIER. The other stages describe the
progression; do not depict them or borrow their decorations for an earlier tier.

1 — BEGINNER
Soft blue-gray or muted lavender. Simple layered rim and seal. Minimal shading
and decoration. No ribbon, glow, radial rays, sparkles outside the central emblem,
crown or gemstone. The sparkle inside a family's central symbol is still allowed.

2 — LEARNER
Fresh mint and green colors. A slightly richer double rim, brighter seal and
cleaner highlights. No ribbon, glow, radial rays or decorative sparkles.

3 — ACHIEVER
Warm yellow and orange colors. More defined rim and highlights. Add one short,
simple two-tail ribbon beneath the medallion. No glow, rays or floating sparkles.

4 — EXPERT
Vivid blue and cyan colors. A larger two-tail ribbon, refined border and a few
short, low-opacity blue radial rays immediately behind the badge. Effects are
subtle, stay close to the medallion and do not obscure the central symbol.

5 — MASTER
Rich violet and purple colors. Enhanced ribbon and layered rim, stronger clean
highlights and a restrained violet halo. Add two or three small decorative
sparkles around the rim, clearly separated from the central achievement icon.

6 — LEGEND
Celebratory gold outer rim with harmonious magenta, pink and cyan accents.
Premium layered ribbon and luminous inner seal. Use a controlled colored glow,
short radial rays and a few small sparkles. Make the prestige clear through the
frame and ribbon; keep the central symbol unchanged and unobscured. Avoid a large
cloud of effects, rainbow clutter, or entirely new badge construction.

COMPOSITION AND READABILITY
Square image, one centered badge, front view, no tilt or perspective scene.
Keep the complete medallion, ribbon and any allowed effects inside the canvas
with generous clear padding. Preserve consistent medal body scale and alignment
across the family; higher-tier accessories must not force the core icon to shrink.
The emblem should occupy most of the inner seal and remain recognizable at
48–64 pixels. Avoid tiny details, thin decorative lines and complex textures.
Use silhouettes, ribbon stages and border changes as well as color to signal tier.

BACKGROUND AND EXCLUSIONS
Use a uniform PURE WHITE background outside the badge, with no scenery, ground
plane, checkerboard, background gradient or broad cast shadow. Colored glow is
allowed only for the stages that specify it and must fade into clean white.
No readable words, letters, numbers, labels, headings, UI, phone mockups, extra
badges or comparison chart. No photorealism, realistic metal, realistic fabric,
dark fantasy elements, intricate filigree or excessive 3D rendering.

FINAL CHECK
Generate exactly ONE badge for FAMILY and REQUESTED TIER. Keep its achievement
identity recognizable. Do not include its name, tier name or numeric requirement
in the artwork; the app renders those separately.
```

## Upgrading an established family

When generating tiers 2–6, attach the selected Beginner badge as the identity
reference. Attach a previously selected target-tier badge from another family
only when needed to establish consistent frame treatment. Identify those roles
explicitly in the request. Keep the base reference for every stage, rather than
using only the previous tier and accumulating drift.

Append this instruction to the filled prompt:

```text
The attached [FAMILY] Beginner badge is the identity reference. Create only its
[REQUESTED TIER] upgrade. Preserve the central emblem's exact silhouette, stroke
weight, position, relative size, and the medallion body's dimensions. Change
only the tier palette, frame richness, highlights, ribbon and effects allowed
for the requested stage. Do not redraw the family into a different concept.
```

## First Word / Beginner: concise ready-to-use prompt

```text
Create ONE standalone KasiGuru achievement badge: Word Explorer, milestone First
Word, Tier 1 — Beginner. Use the attached six-badge image as the construction and
cartoon-style reference, especially its simplest upper-left medallion.

Draw a front-facing circular medallion in soft blue-gray and muted lavender,
with a thick outer rim, secondary inner ring and softly scalloped inner seal.
Center a large cream-colored rounded speech bubble containing one simple
four-point sparkle. This emblem represents speaking and learning a first word.
Use smooth layered cartoon shapes, clean contours and subtle upper-left highlights.
Keep the emblem bold and readable at 48–64 pixels.

Tier 1 is simple: no ribbon, glow, rays, decorative sparkles outside the speech
bubble, crown, gemstone or extra ornament. The sparkle inside the speech bubble
is part of the achievement identity and must remain.

Use a square image with one centered badge, generous padding and a pure white
background. No text, letters, numbers, labels, characters, scenery, UI, additional
badges or tier grid. Avoid realistic metal, detailed textures and excessive 3D.

Establish a clear base design whose exact central symbol, medallion proportions
and icon placement can be retained when its frame later evolves through Learner,
Achiever, Expert, Master and Legend. Generate ONLY this Beginner badge.
```

## App export

The prompts above preserve the user's pure-white output request. For production
assets, change only the background instruction to “genuinely transparent
background outside the badge; no white rectangle or checkerboard,” and request
transparent output through the image-generation tool. Preserve the alpha in PNG
or WebP. Do not ship a white square behind a badge on the app's dark background.

Keep labels, numbers, requirements and progress in Compose. Inspect every asset
at small sizes and compare the family silhouette across tiers before integrating.
