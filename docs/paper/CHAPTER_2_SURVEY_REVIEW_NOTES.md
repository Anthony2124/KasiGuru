# Chapter 2 survey alignment notes

Survey reviewed: https://forms.gle/Q2x41EDiyDMj2TrP8

The chapter draft describes the public questionnaire, not private response data. No response counts or results were available from the respondent link. Tourism Casiguran is the beneficiary and outside-Casiguran participation was confirmed by the researcher.

## Confirm before submission

- Total valid respondents and counts by administration format and location.
- Collection dates and actual venues for paper administration.
- Actual recruitment and sampling process.
- Application version and how respondents accessed or used it before rating.
- Whether paper questions exactly matched the online instrument.
- Evidence of any instrument review, pilot testing, institutional permission, and consent arrangements for minors.
- Approved interpretation ranges for mean scores, section completion rules, overall-score weighting, and data retention arrangements.

## Alignment findings

- The existing manuscript adopts six characteristics from ISO/IEC 25010:2011. The actual form names ISO/IEC 25010:2023 and contains nine quality sections, including Safety. The chapter draft follows the actual form. Chapter 1 research questions, objectives, framework, and references need reconciliation with this change.
- The form contains 38 quality-rating statements, nine profile questions, and two optional open-ended feedback questions.
- The four response labels are Highly Functional (4), Functional (3), Needs Improvement (2), and Poor (1). They are not agreement labels.
- Several Functional Suitability, Interaction Capability, Flexibility, and Safety items refer to report submission, decision-making, institutional planning, or replacement of a reporting/decision-support system. Compatibility also refers to other systems at ASCOT. These references may not assess the functions of a Kasiguranin learning application.
- The form's introduction lists eight quality characteristics, while the body contains nine. Safety appears in the body but is omitted from the introductory list.
- The general questionnaire does not contain a separate acceptability or satisfaction scale, although it includes engagement-related items and open-ended experience feedback. Do not claim it separately measures all acceptability or satisfaction constructs without identifying the applicable items.
- Some technical questions ask respondents to assess matters they may not have observed, such as resistance to attacks, component reuse, or recoverability. Technical evidence and evaluator qualifications should inform interpretation.
- The form requires an email address. Raw responses are confidential but are not anonymous.
- The questionnaire lists minors among its age categories. The public form alone does not establish parental/guardian consent or participant assent.
- The household-language question permits multiple selections; the percentage total may exceed 100 percent.

## Preserve collected data

Do not silently change the wording associated with answers already collected. If the questionnaire is revised, preserve the original instrument and response export, document the new version and its collection dates, and retain a version indicator in the analysis dataset. The public form was inspected without submitting an answer or changing it.
