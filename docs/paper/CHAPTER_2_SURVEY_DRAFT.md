# Chapter 2

# RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

This chapter presents the research design, locale of the study, respondents, data gathering instrument, data gathering procedure, data analysis technique, and ethical concerns of the study. It describes the development and evaluation of KasiGuru, a gamified mobile learning application for the preservation and learning of the Kasiguranin language. Tourism Casiguran is the intended beneficiary. The study gathers evaluation responses through Google Forms and printed questionnaires with paper answer sheets, including online responses from users outside Casiguran.

## Research Design

The study uses a descriptive-developmental research design. The developmental component concerns the creation and improvement of KasiGuru through the Phased Development Approach, which includes requirements, analysis, design, coding or implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance. Development records document the activities and outputs of these phases. The descriptive component summarizes respondents' assessments of the application and the suggestions they provide for its improvement.

The administered evaluation questionnaire is adapted from the ISO/IEC 25010:2023 product quality model. Its rating sections cover functional suitability, performance efficiency, compatibility, interaction capability, reliability, security, maintainability, flexibility, and safety. The 2023 standard defines a product quality model with nine characteristics ([ISO, 2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/78176.html)). Questionnaire scores describe respondents' perceptions and must be interpreted in relation to their experience and qualifications. Technical conclusions require supporting testing evidence. The study does not measure a causal effect on language proficiency or long-term language preservation.

## Locale of the Study

The study is centered on Casiguran, Aurora, Philippines, the community associated with the Kasiguranin language and the location of Tourism Casiguran, the intended beneficiary. The application is intended to support access to Kasiguranin learning material and digital documentation relevant to the community's language and cultural heritage.

The respondent coverage extends beyond the municipality because the Google Forms questionnaire has also received answers from users outside Casiguran. The form distinguishes participants in the Poblacion area, participants in non-Poblacion areas, and participants outside Casiguran. The respondents are individuals who answer the online or paper-based evaluation questionnaire. They include participants from Casiguran and participants outside the municipality. The questionnaire allows respondents to identify as students, educators, community members, language or heritage advocates, IT professionals, or another profile. These are available response categories; the final report will state which groups actually participated.

Recruitment will be described using [ACTUAL SAMPLING OR RECRUITMENT METHOD], based on how participants were invited and any eligibility screening applied. Tourism Casiguran's role as beneficiary is separate from participation in the survey. Questionnaire results will represent the participating respondents rather than being presented as the views of the tourism office or all Casiguran residents. Respondent profile information will also help distinguish general user assessments from ratings submitted by respondents identifying as IT professionals.

## Data Gathering Instrument

The main instrument is the researcher-prepared [KasiGuru evaluation questionnaire](https://forms.gle/Q2x41EDiyDMj2TrP8), administered through Google Forms and a printed questionnaire with a paper answer sheet. Its introductory portion explains the evaluation purpose and includes a data privacy notice and informed-consent statement.

The questionnaire has three principal components. The respondent profile component collects an email address, role or profile, native-language status, language or languages used at home, age group, sex or gender response, location, familiarity with mobile games or educational applications, and device information. The software evaluation component contains 38 rating statements organized into nine quality sections. The feedback component contains two open-ended questions about the application's strengths, weaknesses, and possible additional features.

| Quality section | Number of rating items |
| --- | ---: |
| Functional suitability | 3 |
| Performance efficiency | 3 |
| Compatibility | 2 |
| Interaction capability | 7 |
| Reliability | 4 |
| Security | 6 |
| Maintainability | 4 |
| Flexibility | 4 |
| Safety | 5 |
| **Total** | **38** |

Each rating statement uses four response choices: **4 — Highly Functional; 3 — Functional; 2 — Needs Improvement; and 1 — Poor**. These are the labels used in the administered form and will be retained in the analysis.

The online and printed versions must be checked for equivalent wording, instructions, and response choices before their scores are combined. Any differences will be documented. The questionnaire is an adaptation for this study rather than a claim of complete ISO certification. Some current items refer to reporting, decision support, or institutional systems; their relevance to KasiGuru must be considered when interpreting the ratings. Any revisions will be recorded as a new instrument version, with already collected responses retaining the wording originally answered. Instrument review or pilot testing will be reported only where supported by actual records.

## Data Gathering Procedure

The data gathering procedure includes explaining the study's purpose, providing consent information, and distributing the questionnaire through online and paper-based channels. The Google Forms link reaches participants within and outside Casiguran. Printed questionnaires and paper answer sheets provide a physical response format. The researchers will record the collection dates, paper-survey venues, application version, and how respondents accessed or tried the application. Receipt of a completed questionnaire alone will not be treated as proof of supervised application use.

Respondents are asked to provide their own assessments and submit only one response to the same evaluation. Researchers may clarify instructions without suggesting ratings. Any assistance or interruptions relevant to the evaluation will be documented. Where participants are minors, the required consent and assent arrangements must be addressed before their responses are included in the study.

Google Forms responses will be exported to a spreadsheet. Answers from paper sheets will be entered using the same item names and numerical codes and checked against the original sheets. The combined dataset will retain the administration format and respondent location. Any identifying information needed for response checking will be kept separate from the analytical dataset. Potential duplicates, incomplete records, and withdrawn responses will be handled using documented criteria. Original online records and paper sheets will be retained securely for verification.

## Data Analysis Technique

Descriptive statistics will summarize the respondents' profiles and evaluation answers. Frequency counts and percentages will describe profile categories, survey format, and the distribution of each rating. The percentage for a category will be calculated as **P = (f / N) × 100**, where **f** is the category frequency and **N** is the relevant number of valid responses. For questions allowing multiple selections, percentages will be identified as multiple-response percentages and may total more than 100 percent. Missing answers will be reported separately.

The weighted mean for each quality item will be calculated as **WM = Σ(f × w) / n**, where **f** is the frequency of a rating category, **w** is its weight from 1 to 4, and **n** is the number of valid scored answers to that item. An unanswered item will not receive a score of zero. Response distributions will accompany mean scores.

The following interpretation ranges are proposed for mean scores rounded to two decimal places and remain subject to adviser confirmation:

| Mean score | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| 3.26–4.00 | Highly Functional |
| 2.51–3.25 | Functional |
| 1.76–2.50 | Needs Improvement |
| 1.00–1.75 | Poor |

Quality-section summaries will state the number of items and valid respondents contributing to each result. Because the sections contain different numbers of items, any overall score will identify whether it weights individual items equally or gives each section equal weight. Ratings may also be presented by respondent location or profile when group sizes permit meaningful description. Responses from different questionnaire versions will remain identifiable and will not be pooled without checking their comparability.

The two open-ended answers will be reviewed and grouped into recurring themes concerning strengths, problems, and proposed improvements. These comments will help explain the numerical ratings. The findings will describe the participating sample and its evaluation conditions. User perceptions alone will not establish technical security, maintainability, educational effectiveness, or full conformity with the ISO standard.

## Ethical Concerns

Participation is voluntary. The questionnaire includes a privacy notice and consent statement explaining the research purpose, confidentiality of responses, and the participant's right to withdraw without penalty. Necessary institutional permissions and any required ethics clearance must be documented. For minors, parental or guardian consent and age-appropriate participant assent must be arranged where required; the general online consent statement will not be described as proof that these arrangements were completed.

The online questionnaire requires an email address, so its raw responses are identifiable and will be treated as confidential rather than anonymous. Email addresses and other identifying details will be separated from the analytical dataset wherever possible. Electronic records will be stored with restricted access, and printed answer sheets will be kept in a secure location. The research team will specify authorized access, the retention period, and the disposal arrangements. Reports will use aggregate results and anonymized excerpts.

The form's privacy notice refers to Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 ([National Privacy Commission](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/)). The researchers must follow the applicable institutional data-handling requirements and accurately describe the safeguards actually implemented. Results will acknowledge nonlocal participation, preserve the original response record, and disclose instrument limitations. Kasiguranin content will be handled with respect for source attribution, contributor permission, and cultural and linguistic integrity.
