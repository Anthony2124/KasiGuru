Casiguran, Aurora — Kasiguranin is an endangered Northern Philippine language, and it is not taught in schools. KasiGuru is a free Android app that teaches it the way you would actually learn it: a word at a time, checked by the people who still speak it.

Why it matters

A language is not lost all at once. It goes one word at a time.

Kasiguranin is spoken in Casiguran, Aurora, and the people who speak it best are the oldest people in town. KasiGuru exists to put the language somewhere it can be found later — by a child in Casiguran, or by a grandchild somewhere else entirely. The app is free and always will be, and the dictionary is open whether or not you ever install it.
