Why it matters

A language is not lost all at once. It goes one word at a time.

Kasiguranin is spoken in Casiguran, Aurora, and the people who speak it best are the oldest people in town. It is not taught in schools. KasiGuru puts the language somewhere it can be found later, by a child in Casiguran or by a grandchild somewhere else entirely.

  • Checked by speakersEntries come from published documentation and from speakers in Casiguran. Words the community sends in are checked by a moderator before they join the dictionary.
  • Written the way it soundsEntries carry IPA pronunciation, the word's root, and its meaning in Tagalog and English.
  • Practice, not flashcardsShort lessons, games, streaks and XP, so words get used instead of only read.
  • Free, and staying freeNo ads, and no account needed to start learning.
