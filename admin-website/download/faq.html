Frequently Asked Questions

What is KasiGuru?

KasiGuru is a gamified mobile learning app for the Kasiguranin language, spoken in Casiguran, Aurora, Philippines. It's part of a thesis initiative to preserve the language.

Is KasiGuru free?

Yes, completely free. There are no in-app purchases.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

As a thesis project, we distribute directly via APK. See our install guide for step-by-step instructions.

What Android version do I need?

Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher.

Is my data safe?

KasiGuru uses Firebase for secure authentication and data storage. Your progress syncs across devices.

Can I contribute words?

Yes! The app lets you submit Kasiguranin words from the community. Submissions are reviewed by administrators before entering the dictionary.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Contact us through the GitHub repository or reach out to the project team.

What are the six game modes?

Multiple Choice, True/False, Fill in the Blanks, Matching, Word Scramble, and Listening exercises.
