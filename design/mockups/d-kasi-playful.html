Why it matters

A language is not lost all at once. It goes one word at a time.

Kasiguranin is spoken in Casiguran, Aurora, and the people who speak it best are the oldest people in town. It is not taught in schools. KasiGuru exists to put the language somewhere it can be found later, by a child in Casiguran or by a grandchild somewhere else entirely.

Verified by speakers

Entries come from published thesis documentation and from speakers in Casiguran. Community submissions are checked by a moderator before they join the corpus.

IPA transcription

Every entry carries phonetic notation, grammatical root tags, and both English and Filipino definitions.

Practice, not flashcards

Streaks, XP and six game modes, so the words get used rather than only read.

Free, and staying free
