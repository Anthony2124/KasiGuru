How it works

A path, not a word list

Each day gives you a short lesson, a review of what is slipping, a game and a story — built from the words you have actually met.

01

Today's path

Four steps, ten minutes. The app decides what to review based on what you keep forgetting, not on where you left off.

02

Games that test recall

Matching, listening and recall drills turn a vocabulary list into something you recognise when a neighbour says it out loud.

03

Add what is missing

Know a word the dictionary does not have? Submit it. A moderator in Casiguran verifies it and it joins the record.

Why it matters

A language is not lost all at once

Kasiguranin sits between Tagalog and the Northern Luzon languages, and it is not taught in schools. The people who speak it best are the oldest people in town. Every entry here was collected from them, checked, and published so a child in Casiguran can still find it in twenty years. The app is free and always will be.

Reserved for real voices

Testimonials from actual users, teachers and elders belong here. Left deliberately empty — Adrian supplies the real quotes, the same way he supplies the artwork. Nothing invented ships.
