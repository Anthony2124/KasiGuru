How it works

01

Learn a path, not a list

Each day gives you a short lesson, a review of what is slipping, a game, and a story — built from the words you have actually met.

02

Play until it sticks

Matching, listening and recall games turn a vocabulary list into something you can recognise when a neighbour says it out loud.

03

Add what is missing

Know a word the dictionary does not? Submit it. A moderator in Casiguran verifies it and it joins the record for everyone.

Why it matters

“A language is not lost all at once. It goes one word at a time.”

Kasiguranin sits between Tagalog and the Northern Luzon languages, and it is not taught in schools. The people who speak it best are the oldest people in town. Every entry in this dictionary was collected from them, checked, and published so that a child in Casiguran — or a grandchild somewhere else entirely — can still find it in twenty years.

The app is free and always will be. The dictionary is open to anyone, whether or not they ever install it.
